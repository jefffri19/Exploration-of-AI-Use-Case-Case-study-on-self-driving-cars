# Exploration-of-AI-Use-Case-Case-study-on-self-driving-cars

# AIM
To study the application of Artificial Intelligence (AI) in self-driving cars and understand how AI technologies help vehicles perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate safely without continuous human control.

# THEORY

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as perception, learning, decision-making, and problem-solving.

A self-driving car, also known as an autonomous vehicle, uses AI technologies, sensors, cameras, and computer systems to navigate roads and perform driving tasks with reduced or no continuous human input, depending on its level of automation.

AI plays an important role in helping self-driving cars understand their surroundings, detect obstacles, identify traffic signs, recognize pedestrians, and select safe driving actions.

### Major AI technologies used in self-driving cars

1\. Computer Vision: Processes images from cameras to identify roads, lane markings, traffic lights, vehicles, and pedestrians.

2\. Machine Learning (ML): Enables the vehicle to learn patterns from driving data and improve object recognition and prediction.

3\. Sensor Fusion: Combines data from cameras, radar, LiDAR, GPS, and other sensors to build a more reliable understanding of the surroundings.

4\. Decision-Making Algorithms: Analyse road conditions and select appropriate actions, such as braking, accelerating, changing lanes, or stopping.

5\. Path Planning and Navigation: Uses maps, GPS, and environmental information to determine a suitable route and plan a safe path.

6\. Control Systems: Convert driving decisions into steering, braking, and acceleration commands to control the vehicle's movement.

# Working Principle

The working principle of an AI-based self-driving car involves the following steps:

1\. Data collection: Cameras and sensors collect information about the road, nearby vehicles, pedestrians, and obstacles.

2\. Environment perception: Computer vision and AI algorithms analyse the collected data to identify objects, traffic signs, lane markings, and road conditions.

3\. Sensor fusion: Data from multiple sensors are combined to improve the vehicle's understanding of its position and surroundings.

4\. Decision-making: The AI system evaluates the driving situation and determines the appropriate action.

5\. Path planning: The navigation system calculates a safe route and selects a suitable path while considering obstacles and traffic conditions.

6\. Vehicle control: The control system operates steering, brakes, and acceleration according to the planned movement.

7\. Continuous monitoring: The vehicle repeatedly senses its surroundings and updates its decisions as road conditions change.

# RESULT

The study demonstrates that Artificial Intelligence plays a significant role in the operation of self-driving cars. AI technologies enable autonomous vehicles to detect and recognize objects, understand road conditions, make driving decisions, and navigate along planned routes.

Thus, the objective of understanding how AI helps self-driving cars perceive their surroundings, make decisions, and navigate with reduced continuous human control is achieved. However, safe operation depends on the vehicle's automation level, sensor reliability, road conditions, and the limitations of its autonomous driving system.
